Useful to know

What do the assessments measure?

They are original self-reflection questionnaires for career exploration. They are not validated aptitude tests.

What jobs does the built-in feed include?

Recent remote listings. Available job-search tools in chat can support broader searches.

Can I open the current workspace?

The current private release requires the owner account. Review access before sharing this template publicly.
