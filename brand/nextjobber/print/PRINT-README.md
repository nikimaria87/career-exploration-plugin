# Business card printing

Trim: 3.5 × 2 inches. Artwork: 3.75 × 2.25 inches, including 0.125-inch bleed on every edge. Raster exports are 1125 × 675 pixels for 300 ppi at bleed size. Keep content inside the safe area. Replace bracketed contact fields before printing. Confirm the QR destination and dashboard access.

The supplied PDFs use RGB artwork. Ask your printer to apply its preferred CMYK conversion/profile and trim settings. SVG masters remain editable.
