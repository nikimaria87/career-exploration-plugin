# NextJobber brand guidelines

## Identity
NextJobber helps independent job seekers explore career options, compare opportunities, prepare applications, and plan skill development.

The original wordmark uses full E and B stems, rounded stroke joins, rebalanced widths, and a custom NJ symbol. The reference informed the airy geometric character and two-tone treatment. The supplied artwork is newly drawn.

## Logo use
Use the primary two-tone wordmark on white or paper. Use the white version on plum. Keep clear space of half the capital-letter height around the logo. Minimum wordmark width: 180 screen pixels or 45 mm in print. Below that, use the NJ symbol at 24 pixels or larger. Keep its proportions and stroke weights.

## Palette
Plum #4C1734 is the primary brand color. Warm gray #766B70 is secondary. Ink #241C24 is the main body text. Rose #E3B4C6 is a decorative accent. Paper #FBF7F9 is the light background. Use ink or plum for small text on light backgrounds and white for text on plum. Use rose for accents rather than small text on white.

## Typography
The wordmark is custom outlined artwork. Use DejaVu Sans for editable body copy and DejaVu Sans Bold for headings. Font files and the redistribution license are included.

## Voice
Use clear, practical, encouraging language. Describe the user’s choices and next action. Explain how examples support a job comparison. Avoid claims of guaranteed interviews, validated aptitude scores, or universal ATS scores.

## Templates
Replace bracketed contact and invoice fields before use. The social canvases are supplied at the named dimensions with editable SVG text. Preview platform cropping before posting. The QR points to the current owner-private dashboard and requires a destination/access review before public printing.

## Motion
A short, gentle reveal introduces the symbol and wordmark. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Keep a still version wherever the animation appears.

## Service features
The studio provides local brand settings, exports, and a simple design editor. Enterprise Chat is a support-widget design and response library, not a staffed support service.
